# Centrifugal Process Pump Manual (Model CP-200)

## Normal operating limits
Bearing temperature normal up to 75 °C; warning at 75 °C and alarm at 90 °C. Vibration velocity above 4.5 mm/s is a warning and above 7.1 mm/s an alarm. Discharge pressure should stay above 3.0 bar at duty point; 2.0 bar or less is an alarm. Flow below 80 percent of rated is a warning and below 60 percent an alarm. Motor current normal up to 40 A.

## Fault: Cavitation
Symptoms: crackling or gravel noise, vibration, fluctuating discharge pressure, low flow, noise gets worse when the suction valve is throttled. Common causes: low suction tank level, blocked suction strainer, partially closed suction valve, high fluid temperature, air entering suction line. Inspection: check tank level and NPSH margin, clean the suction strainer, confirm the suction valve is fully open, look for air leaks at the suction flange and shaft seal.

## Fault: Mechanical seal leakage
Symptoms: leak at the shaft, dripping from the seal housing, wet baseplate, seal flush line blocked, smell of product. Common causes: worn seal faces, dry running, shaft misalignment, flush plan failure, excessive vibration. Inspection: measure leak rate, check flush line flow, check shaft runout and alignment, inspect for dry running events. Follow product safety data sheet when handling leaked fluid.

## Fault: Bearing overheating
Symptoms: bearing temperature alarm, hot bearing housing, burnt grease smell, rising vibration. Common causes: over-greasing or wrong grease, lack of lubrication, misalignment, contaminated bearing, excessive axial load from worn impeller wear rings. Inspection: check grease type and quantity, check alignment with a dial indicator or laser, take vibration spectrum, feel for housing temperature rise compared with the other pumps, check bearing seals.

## Fault: Low flow or low discharge pressure
Symptoms: flow below rated, pressure drop, motor current low, pump runs but delivers little. Common causes: worn impeller, wear ring clearance too large, wrong rotation direction after electrical work, discharge valve partly closed, blocked impeller, suction problems as in cavitation. Inspection: verify rotation direction, compare head against the pump curve, check valve positions, open the casing to inspect the impeller if the cause is not found.

## Fault: Impeller wear ring clearance and internal recirculation
Symptoms: gradual loss of discharge head and delivery volume, motor current lower than normal at shut-off, high internal recirculation noise. Common causes: abrasive particulate in pumped fluid, chemical erosion, prolonged operation near shut-off head, cavitation pitting on blade leading edges. Inspection: measure axial and radial shaft clearances, disassemble casing and measure front and rear wear ring gap with feeler gauge (replace rings if clearance > 2x design), examine impeller vanes for erosion channels.

## Fault: Shaft misalignment and pipe strain
Symptoms: high 1X and 2X rotational vibration, premature bearing failure on pump drive-end or motor drive-end, coupling elastomer insert shredded, hot coupling guard. Common causes: cold alignment performed without thermal growth compensation, pipe strain pulling on pump casing flanges, soft foot under motor or pump feet. Inspection: perform dial indicator or laser shaft alignment check across coupling hubs, loosen flange bolts to check for pipe strain deflection (> 0.05 mm indicates strain), check and shim soft foot.

## Fault: Dry running and suction line air ingress
Symptoms: sudden loss of prime, zero flow, rapid temperature spike in seal chamber, squealing from dry silicon carbide faces. Common causes: suction sump emptied, vortex formation in shallow tank, leaking suction pipe gasket under negative pressure, stuck foot valve. Inspection: inspect suction pit submergence depth, verify vortex breaker installed, pressurize suction line to detect air intake points, replace burnt mechanical seal faces.

## Maintenance intervals
Grease bearings every 2000 hours; alignment check every 6 months; seal inspection monthly; vibration route measurement monthly. Isolate, lock out and drain the pump before any work.
