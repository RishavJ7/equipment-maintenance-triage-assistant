# Rotary Screw Compressor Manual (Model RSC-75)

## Normal operating limits
Discharge temperature normal range is 80-110 °C; warning at 110 °C and alarm at 130 °C. Discharge pressure normal 7-9.5 bar; trip at 11 bar. Oil pressure must stay above 2.0 bar; below 1.5 bar is an alarm. Motor current normal up to 95 A. Vibration velocity above 4.5 mm/s needs attention and above 7.1 mm/s is an alarm.

## Fault: High discharge temperature
Symptoms: temperature alarm, high temperature trip, compressor shuts down on high temp, hot air at outlet. Common causes: blocked or dirty oil cooler, low oil level, failed thermostatic valve, clogged oil filter, high ambient temperature or poor room ventilation, cooling fan failure. Inspection: check oil level in the sight glass, inspect cooler fins for dust, confirm cooling fan rotation, check thermostatic valve operation, read the oil filter differential pressure.

## Fault: Low oil pressure
Symptoms: low oil pressure alarm, trip after startup, whining noise on start, oil pressure drops under load. Common causes: low oil level, clogged oil filter, worn oil pump, minimum pressure valve fault, oil leak. Inspection: check oil level when stopped, inspect for leaks at fittings and the filter housing, replace the oil filter if differential pressure is above 1 bar, listen for pump noise. Do not restart repeatedly with low oil pressure because the airend can be damaged.

## Fault: Frequent tripping and short cycling
Symptoms: compressor trips repeatedly, starts and stops often, trips twice in a day, trips after restart. Common causes: overload from high motor current, pressure switch or sensor drift, leaking air system causing short cycling, loose electrical connections, contactor wear, voltage imbalance. Inspection: record motor current on all phases, tighten and thermally scan terminals, check pressure sensor calibration, check receiver drain and air leaks.

## Fault: Abnormal noise and vibration
Symptoms: loud noise, grinding, rattling, knocking after restart, high vibration. Common causes: worn motor or airend bearings, loose mounting bolts or anti-vibration mounts, coupling misalignment or wear, loss of lubrication. Inspection: check mounting bolts, inspect coupling and its insert, take vibration readings at motor DE/NDE and airend, check bearing temperature, listen with a stethoscope. A grinding noise combined with rising vibration suggests bearing damage; stop the unit if vibration exceeds 7.1 mm/s.

## Fault: Air-oil separator element differential pressure
Symptoms: high pressure drop across separator, oil carryover into plant air lines, high oil consumption, compressor trips on discharge pressure. Common causes: blinded separator element, degraded or oxidized compressor oil, operating at excessive discharge temperatures, incorrect oil grade. Inspection: check separator differential pressure gauge (replace element if dP > 0.8 bar), inspect downstream air receiver drain for oil accumulation, inspect scavenger line and orifice for dirt or blockages.

## Fault: Intake unloader and minimum pressure valve failure
Symptoms: compressor fails to build pressure, fails to load or unload, continuous blow-off at idle, motor stays at full load current during unload. Common causes: stuck unloader piston, split control air tubing, faulty load solenoid valve, worn seals in minimum pressure valve (MPV), moisture contamination in pilot lines. Inspection: inspect unloader valve movement on load/unload command, test load solenoid coil operation, check pilot air filter, inspect MPV check valve spring and seat.

## Fault: External oil leak and pipe joint fracture
Symptoms: oil pooling beneath unit, dripping from cooler pipe unions, low oil level in reservoir, oil mist in enclosure. Common causes: loose SAE flange bolts due to vibration, hardened or cracked O-rings, fatigue crack on rigid discharge pipe, leaking shaft lip seal. Inspection: wipe down unit and perform leak detection run, torque flange fasteners to OEM specification, inspect flexible hoses for hardening or outer braid damage.

## Maintenance intervals
Oil filter every 2000 hours; oil every 4000 hours; air filter every 2000 hours; separator element every 4000 hours; coupling inspection every 4000 hours; lubricate motor bearings per nameplate. Always lock out and tag out and depressurise the system before opening any component.
