# Belt Conveyor Manual (Model BC-12, gearbox driven)

## Normal operating limits
Gearbox oil temperature normal up to 80 °C; alarm at 95 °C. Drive motor current normal up to 30 A; alarm at 38 A. Belt speed below 90 percent of nominal is a warning and below 80 percent an alarm. Vibration velocity above 4.5 mm/s warning, above 7.1 mm/s alarm.

## Fault: Belt slip
Symptoms: belt speed drops under load, squealing at the drive pulley, smell of burning rubber, polished lagging, drive pulley turning faster than belt. Common causes: low belt tension, worn or wet pulley lagging, overload or material surge, take-up system stuck. Inspection: check the take-up position and tension, inspect pulley lagging, check load on the belt, compare belt speed with drive speed. Burning smell can indicate friction heat; treat as a fire risk and inspect before restart.

## Fault: Belt mistracking
Symptoms: belt drifts to one side, belt edge damage, material spillage, tracking switch trips. Common causes: misaligned idlers or pulleys, material build-up on rollers, uneven loading, frame out of square. Inspection: walk the belt and check idler alignment, clean build-up, check loading point, adjust training idlers in small steps.

## Fault: Gearbox overheating
Symptoms: gearbox oil temperature alarm, oil leak at seals, oil smell, increased noise. Common causes: oil level too low or too high, wrong oil grade, blocked breather, cooling fan fouled, overload, worn gears or bearings. Inspection: check oil level and condition, clean breather and fan, check motor current for overload, take an oil sample for metal particles.

## Fault: Roller or idler bearing failure
Symptoms: squealing or rumbling from a roller, seized roller, hot roller, vibration along the conveyor. Common causes: contaminated bearing, worn seals, dust build-up, lack of grease. Inspection: use an infrared thermometer along the idler line, listen with a stethoscope, replace seized rollers promptly because a seized roller can cut the belt.

## Fault: Gravity take-up and tension trolley binding
Symptoms: belt sag between carrying idlers, drive pulley spin during start, counterweight hanging tilted or caught in guides, uneven belt stretch. Common causes: take-up carriage guide rollers rusted or seized, wire rope damaged or jumped off sheave, material spillage jamming counterweight tower. Inspection: inspect vertical tower guides for alignment and rust, verify free vertical movement of carriage, check wire rope condition and sheave bearings, verify correct counterweight mass per engineering sheet.

## Fault: Chute blockage and skirting seal damage
Symptoms: motor current spiking, conveyor stalling, material pile-up at transfer chute, heavy dust generation, belt scored longitudinally. Common causes: oversized material bridging chute throat, high moisture fines sticking to chute walls, chute level probe failure, hardened skirting pinching rocks. Inspection: inspect chute wear liners and clean accumulation, test plug chute tilt switch, inspect skirtboard rubber clearance (maintain 2-3 mm clearance from belt, never clamp tight into moving carcass).

## Fault: Drive pulley lagging wear and debonding
Symptoms: rubber strips peeling off drive pulley, metal-to-rubber squeal, uneven traction leading to cyclic speed fluctuation. Common causes: abrasive material ingress between belt and pulley, continuous belt slip generating frictional heat, adhesive failure between rubber and steel shell. Inspection: inspect pulley surface for missing ceramic tiles or vulcanized rubber tiles, measure remaining lagging thickness, clean self-cleaning return spirals.

## Maintenance intervals
Idler inspection weekly; gearbox oil change every 6 months; belt tension check monthly; lagging inspection quarterly. Lock out the drive before any work near moving parts.
