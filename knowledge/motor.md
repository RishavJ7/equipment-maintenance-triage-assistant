# Three-Phase Induction Motor Manual (Frame 280, 55 kW)

## Normal operating limits
Winding temperature class F: normal up to 120 °C, alarm at 140 °C. Bearing temperature normal up to 80 °C, alarm at 95 °C. Vibration velocity above 4.5 mm/s warning, above 7.1 mm/s alarm. Phase current imbalance above 5 percent is a warning and above 10 percent an alarm.

## Fault: Winding overheating
Symptoms: winding temperature alarm, thermal protection trip, hot casing, smell of hot varnish, motor trips on overload. Common causes: overload, blocked cooling fan cover or dirty fins, ambient temperature too high, voltage imbalance or low supply voltage, frequent starts, insulation breakdown. Inspection: measure phase currents against nameplate, clean the cooling fan and fins, check supply voltage on all phases, count starts per hour, perform an insulation resistance (megger) test after isolation.

## Fault: Current imbalance
Symptoms: unequal phase currents, humming, hot spots, trips on phase loss, torque loss. Common causes: supply voltage imbalance, loose or corroded terminal connection, blown fuse on one phase, partly shorted winding, contactor with a worn contact. Inspection: measure voltage and current on every phase, thermal scan terminals and contactor, check fuses, measure winding resistance phase to phase.

## Fault: Bearing failure
Symptoms: grinding or screeching noise, high vibration, bearing temperature rise, grease leakage, shaft play. Common causes: lubrication failure, over-greasing, shaft current (VFD fluted bearings), misalignment, belt over-tension, contamination. Inspection: check grease condition, take vibration spectrum at DE and NDE, inspect for fluting signs, check alignment and belt tension, check the shaft earthing ring on VFD-driven motors.

## Fault: Fails to start or hums
Symptoms: motor hums but does not turn, trips at start, slow acceleration. Common causes: single phasing, seized load, failed starter or capacitor, broken rotor bars, low voltage. Inspection: confirm three-phase voltage at terminals, rotate the shaft by hand once isolated, check starter and overload relay settings.

## Fault: Stator insulation degradation and ground fault
Symptoms: earth fault relay trip, breaker trip upon starting, pungent burnt resin odor, motor warm even on light load. Common causes: insulation thermal aging (Class F thermal limit exceeded), moisture ingress into windings, contamination with conductive dust, voltage spikes from variable frequency drive (VFD). Inspection: perform 500V or 1000V Megger insulation resistance test to ground (minimum 100 Megohms acceptable), test polarization index (PI), inspect terminal box for moisture or carbon tracking.

## Fault: Rotor bar fracture or end-ring crack
Symptoms: fluctuating motor current needles, low-frequency slip frequency sideband vibration, reduced starting torque under full load, extended run-up time. Common causes: excessive number of direct-on-line (DOL) starts, thermal fatigue in aluminum or copper cage bars, severe cyclic load oscillations. Inspection: perform motor current signature analysis (MCSA) to measure slip frequency sideband amplitudes, perform rotor single-phase rotation test, examine end rings for cracks.

## Fault: Cooling fan and cowl obstruction
Symptoms: high frame and winding temperatures across all loads, weak airflow from non-drive end cowl, motor trips on thermal overload. Common causes: cooling fan cowl clogged with lint or dust, broken or sheared fan blades on shaft, fan slipping on keyway. Inspection: stop and isolate motor, inspect fan cowl screen, verify fan integrity and keyway retention, blow out stator fin channels with dry compressed air.

## Maintenance intervals
Regrease bearings per nameplate interval; clean cooling path quarterly; insulation resistance test yearly; vibration check monthly. Lock out and tag out before opening the terminal box.
